How DripCut works

Go from one long video to a ready-to-publish content plan

Start by pasting a supported video link or uploading your own file. Choose exact durations for clean sequential clips, or ask DripCut to suggest useful moments when you want help finding the strongest sections. Add readable captions and select portrait, square, or landscape output for the channels you use.

Every result stays under your control. Preview the selected timestamps, keep the clips that fit your message, and download them individually or as a batch. There is no requirement to use AI and no need to rebuild the same crop, caption, and export steps for every short video.

Publishing without the spreadsheet

Approve your clips, schedule the batch, and move on

Connect an eligible YouTube or Instagram account, choose the finished clips, and set the time between posts. DripCut prepares the publishing queue so you can review the channel, caption, privacy setting, date, and time before confirming anything. You can publish to one platform or place the same approved clip on both calendars.

The result is a shorter path from finished long-form video to consistent short-form publishing. Creators keep the decisions that need taste and context while software handles timestamping, repeated exports, filenames, captions, and posting intervals.

Use videos you own or have permission to repurpose
